## A Study of Financial Fraud Detection Practices – Kolon Industries India Private Limite

**MBA Final Year Project (Finance & Business Analytics)**
S.A. College of Arts & Science, University of Madras | Jan 2026 – Mar 2026
Guide: Dr. V. Srinivasan

## 📌 Overview
This study analyses how effective the financial fraud detection and internal control practices are in a manufacturing company. It covers six areas where fraud commonly happens: procurement, inventory, payroll, expense reimbursement, financial reporting and asset management. It also looks at bribery and corruption controls.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyse the effectiveness of fraud detection practices in preventing and controlling fraud
- Examine procurement, inventory, payroll and expense reimbursement controls
- Study financial statement fraud and asset misappropriation controls
- Evaluate the impact of bribery and corruption on fraud detection

## 🔬 Methodology
| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Research design** | Descriptive research |
| **Data collection** | Structured questionnaire built in Google Forms |
| **Sample size** | 136 employees across departments |
| **Sampling method** | Stratified sampling |
| **Secondary data** | Books, journals, websites and earlier studies |
| **Statistical tools** | Percentage Analysis, Chi-Square Test, Correlation, One-Way ANOVA, Simple Linear Regression, Paired Sample T-Test |

## 📊 Key Findings
- Most employees agreed that verification, internal audits, inventory monitoring, payroll controls and financial reporting checks are followed in the organisation
- **Chi-Square:** significant relationship between ethical practices and the effectiveness of internal audits in detecting procurement fraud
- **Correlation:** proper documentation of expense claims improves detection of fake or inflated claims
- **ANOVA:** training and awareness significantly influence management action against corruption
- **Regression:** regular audits reduce the risk of asset misappropriation, though the effect is relatively weak
- **Paired T-Test:** regularly updated inventory records help identify and resolve stock discrepancies faster

## 💡 Recommendations
- Conduct regular training and awareness programmes on ethics and fraud prevention
- Use technology-based auditing and real-time inventory tracking
- Strengthen vendor verification and payroll authorisation controls
- Improve continuous monitoring and reporting systems
